# Requirements Document: Orivon Edge Rebrand

## Introduction

Orivon Edge is undergoing a strategic transformation from a government-focused technology solutions provider to a **hybrid venture studio and digital solutions agency**. This rebrand reflects our dual business model: we build powerful digital solutions for clients while simultaneously incubating, launching, and scaling our own AI-driven startups.

This requirements document outlines the comprehensive website updates needed to communicate this new identity, positioning Orivon Edge as both a client services agency and a startup factory that builds future-proof systems and spins out ventures that solve niche problems worldwide.

## Requirements

### Requirement 1: Update Brand Identity and Core Messaging

**User Story:** As a website visitor, I want to immediately understand that Orivon Edge is a hybrid venture studio and digital agency, so that I can see both their client services and their startup-building capabilities.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a visitor lands on the homepage THEN they SHALL see messaging that clearly positions Orivon Edge as both a venture studio and digital agency
2. WHEN viewing the hero section THEN the system SHALL display value propositions that highlight both client solutions and venture building
3. WHEN reading the tagline THEN it SHALL communicate the dual nature: "We build startups and scale innovation for clients worldwide"
4. IF the visitor reads the about section THEN they SHALL understand that Orivon Edge doesn't just deliver projects but builds future-proof systems and spins out startups
5. WHEN viewing any section THEN the tone SHALL be global, bold, innovation-first, and Gen Z-friendly but professional (not government-heavy)

### Requirement 2: Restructure Services Section with Dual Lanes

**User Story:** As a potential client or investor, I want to see Orivon Edge's services organized into two clear categories (Client Solutions and Venture Studio), so that I understand their complete offering.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN viewing the services section THEN the system SHALL display two distinct service categories: "Client Solutions" and "Venture Studio"
2. WHEN viewing Client Solutions THEN the system SHALL list:
   - Custom Websites (from credibility sites to growth-driven platforms)
   - SaaS Development & Automation
   - AI Agents & Chatbots
   - Digital Transformation for SMEs, NGOs, and governments
3. WHEN viewing Venture Studio THEN the system SHALL list:
   - AI-powered tools (e.g., "ChatGPT for Doctors," "Grammarly for Math," "AI SEO Optimizers")
   - Civic Tech Platforms (e.g., Democrasee, tools for accountability & citizen engagement)
   - Industry-specific SaaS (finance apps, healthcare SaaS, learning platforms)
   - Research-backed prototypes scaled into full companies
4. WHEN reading service descriptions THEN they SHALL emphasize the unique value of the dual model
5. WHEN viewing the services layout THEN it SHALL have a sleek, modern, futuristic AI/startup studio vibe

### Requirement 3: Update About Us Section with New Narrative

**User Story:** As a visitor interested in learning about the company, I want to read an updated About Us section that explains Orivon Edge's evolution and unique positioning, so that I understand what makes them different.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN viewing the About Us section THEN it SHALL state: "Orivon Edge is a global venture studio and technology agency that builds, scales, and launches AI-driven startups while delivering powerful digital solutions for clients"
2. WHEN reading the mission THEN it SHALL emphasize combining product design, automation, and deep research to create impact-driven software
3. WHEN viewing core values THEN they SHALL reflect both client service excellence and startup innovation
4. IF reading the unique value proposition THEN it SHALL clearly state: "Unlike traditional agencies, we don't just deliver projects—we build future-proof systems and spin out startups that solve niche problems worldwide"
5. WHEN viewing the About section THEN it SHALL include messaging about the dual model: "For clients: We give businesses credibility, automation, and growth through technology. For ourselves: We build and scale startups that can be sold or spun off into global ventures"

### Requirement 4: Update Hero Section with New Value Propositions

**User Story:** As a first-time visitor, I want to see compelling hero messaging that immediately communicates Orivon Edge's dual capabilities, so that I'm intrigued to learn more.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the hero section loads THEN the typewriter effect SHALL include new phrases such as:
   - "AI-Driven Venture Studio"
   - "Startup Factory & Digital Agency"
   - "We Build & Scale Startups"
   - "From Prototype to Global Venture"
   - "Innovation-First Solutions"
   - "Venture Building & Client Solutions"
   - "AI Products That Scale"
   - "Research-Backed Startups"
   - "Digital Transformation & Venture Creation"
   - "Building Tomorrow's Companies Today"
2. WHEN reading the hero subtitle THEN it SHALL state something like: "From startups to enterprises, we build AI-driven ventures and deliver scalable technology solutions that create real business impact"
3. WHEN viewing CTAs THEN they SHALL reflect both service lanes (e.g., "Start Your Project" and "Explore Our Ventures")
4. WHEN the hero section displays THEN it SHALL have a global, futuristic, innovation-first visual feel

### Requirement 5: Update Portfolio Section to Showcase Both Client Work and In-House Ventures

**User Story:** As a potential client or investor, I want to see Orivon Edge's portfolio organized to show both client projects and their own startup ventures, so that I can evaluate their full capabilities.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN viewing the portfolio THEN projects SHALL be categorized as either "Client Solutions" or "Orivon Ventures"
2. WHEN viewing Orivon Ventures THEN it SHALL showcase in-house startups like:
   - Democrasee (Civic Tech Platform)
   - FarmAfricaa (AI-powered AgriTech)
   - BICTDA Academy (EdTech Platform)
3. WHEN viewing project descriptions THEN they SHALL emphasize the innovation, AI capabilities, and scalability
4. IF a project is an in-house venture THEN it SHALL be clearly labeled as "Orivon Venture" or "In-House Startup"
5. WHEN filtering projects THEN categories SHALL include: "All", "Client Solutions", "Orivon Ventures", "AI/ML", "SaaS", "Civic Tech"

### Requirement 6: Update Industries Section to Reflect Broader Market Focus

**User Story:** As a business owner or startup founder, I want to see that Orivon Edge serves diverse industries beyond just government, so that I know they can help my specific sector.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN viewing the industries section THEN it SHALL include but not be limited to:
   - Startups & Venture Capital
   - SMEs & Scale-ups
   - Government & Public Sector
   - NGOs & Non-Profits
   - Education & EdTech
   - Healthcare & MedTech
   - Fintech & Banking
   - AI & Automation
2. WHEN reading industry descriptions THEN they SHALL emphasize innovation, scalability, and AI-driven solutions
3. WHEN viewing the industries section THEN government SHALL NOT be the primary focus but one of many sectors served
4. WHEN reading the section header THEN it SHALL say something like "Industries We Transform & Ventures We Build"

### Requirement 7: Update Process Section to Reflect Dual Service Model

**User Story:** As a potential client, I want to understand how Orivon Edge works with clients and how they build their own ventures, so that I know what to expect.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN viewing the process section THEN it SHALL show two tracks: "Client Projects" and "Venture Building"
2. WHEN viewing the Client Projects track THEN it SHALL show steps like:
   - Discovery & Strategy
   - Design & Prototyping
   - Development & Automation
   - Launch & Scale
3. WHEN viewing the Venture Building track THEN it SHALL show steps like:
   - Research & Validation
   - MVP Development
   - Market Testing
   - Scale & Spin-off
4. WHEN reading process descriptions THEN they SHALL emphasize speed, innovation, and future-proof systems

### Requirement 8: Update Testimonials to Include Startup Success Stories

**User Story:** As a visitor evaluating Orivon Edge, I want to see testimonials from both clients and startup founders/investors, so that I can trust their dual capabilities.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN viewing testimonials THEN they SHALL include both client testimonials and venture success stories
2. WHEN reading testimonials THEN some SHALL mention startup building, venture scaling, or AI innovation
3. IF possible THEN testimonials SHALL include metrics like "raised $X in funding" or "scaled to X users"
4. WHEN viewing the testimonials section THEN it SHALL feel global and innovation-focused

### Requirement 9: Update Contact Section with Venture Inquiries Option

**User Story:** As a visitor, I want to contact Orivon Edge for either client services or venture collaboration, so that I can engage with them in the right capacity.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN viewing the contact form THEN it SHALL include an option to select inquiry type: "Client Project", "Venture Collaboration", "Investment Opportunity", "General Inquiry"
2. WHEN submitting a venture-related inquiry THEN the system SHALL route it appropriately
3. WHEN viewing contact messaging THEN it SHALL say something like: "Whether you need a digital solution or want to collaborate on a venture, let's build something extraordinary together"

### Requirement 10: Update Footer with New Positioning

**User Story:** As a visitor, I want the footer to reflect Orivon Edge's new identity, so that the brand message is consistent throughout the site.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN viewing the footer tagline THEN it SHALL say something like: "Building startups and scaling innovation worldwide"
2. WHEN viewing footer links THEN they SHALL include sections for both "Client Services" and "Our Ventures"
3. WHEN reading the footer description THEN it SHALL emphasize the hybrid model: "A global venture studio and digital agency building AI-driven startups and delivering powerful solutions for clients"

### Requirement 11: Update Newsletter Popup with New Lead Magnet

**User Story:** As a website visitor, I want the newsletter popup to offer value relevant to both entrepreneurs and business owners, so that I'm motivated to subscribe.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the newsletter popup appears THEN it SHALL offer a lead magnet like: "The Startup Builder's Toolkit: From Idea to Launch in 90 Days" or "AI Automation Playbook for Modern Businesses"
2. WHEN reading the popup copy THEN it SHALL mention both startup building and client solutions
3. WHEN viewing benefits THEN they SHALL include items like: "Startup building insights", "AI automation tips", "Venture case studies", "Tech trends"

### Requirement 12: Update Navigation to Reflect New Structure

**User Story:** As a visitor, I want the navigation menu to clearly show both service offerings and venture information, so that I can easily find what I'm looking for.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN viewing the navigation THEN it SHALL include menu items like: "Home", "About", "Services", "Our Ventures", "Portfolio", "Contact"
2. IF the Services menu is expandable THEN it SHALL show "Client Solutions" and "Venture Studio" as sub-items
3. WHEN clicking "Our Ventures" THEN it SHALL navigate to a section showcasing in-house startups
4. WHEN viewing mobile navigation THEN it SHALL maintain the same structure in a mobile-friendly format

## Success Metrics

1. Visitor understanding: 90% of surveyed visitors should understand that Orivon Edge is both an agency and venture studio
2. Engagement: 30% increase in time spent on site after rebrand
3. Conversion: 25% increase in contact form submissions with clear intent (client vs. venture)
4. Brand perception: Shift from "government contractor" to "innovation-first startup studio" in visitor surveys
5. Portfolio engagement: 40% increase in clicks on venture projects vs. client projects
